DayCasion Support

We're here to help. Email info@daycasion.com from the address you use for DayCasion and tell us what's happening; screenshots help.

Your account

  • Forgot your password: on the Sign In screen, tap Forgot password and follow the email we send.
  • Change your email or password: Settings > Email & Password.
  • Download your memories: Settings > Privacy & Data > Download My Data, then Export All My Memories. We email you when it's ready; sign in at export.daycasion.com to download it within 7 days. One free export is available every 365 days; if you need another copy sooner, email us.

Plans and purchases

  • Manage or cancel a subscription: Settings > Subscription > Manage Subscription, or your App Store or Google Play subscriptions. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a subscription.
  • New phone or reinstalled the app: sign in and tap Restore Purchases in Settings > Subscription (for Legacy purchases, in Settings > Legacy Executor).
  • Refunds are handled by Apple or Google. See our Subscriptions, Refunds & Digital Products Policy.
  • Need more than five Memory Profiles? Email us with the subject "Additional Memory Profiles".

Adding memories

  • From your phone: Add Memory, or Add Past Memories to bring in many photos and videos at once.
  • From your computer: sign in at import.daycasion.com (Desktop Import) to create memories from folders of photos and videos.
  • From other apps on iPhone: use the Share button and choose DayCasion.
  • Limits: photos up to 50 MB, videos up to 300 MB (any length) and voice recordings up to 30 minutes each, within your plan's storage.

Legacy Executor

If you've been named someone's Legacy Executor, start at legacy.daycasion.com with the Legacy Code you were given. For anything else, including a member who has died without naming an executor, email us.

Account deletion without the app

You can request account deletion by emailing info@daycasion.com from your account email address. See our Account & Data Deletion Policy for the steps and details.
