DayCasion Data Protection & User Rights Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

This policy explains how to see, correct, download and delete your information, and how we handle privacy requests. It works together with our Privacy Policy, which describes what we collect and why.

1. Do it yourself in the app

Request Available method
View memories and profile data Open Profiles, Timeline, Life Stories and Settings
Correct information Edit the relevant memory, profile or Life Story, subject to account access limits
Download information Settings > Privacy & Data > Download my data. The quick export has a media size limit of about 120 MB; contact info@daycasion.com if the archive cannot be completed
Delete selected information Delete a memory, profile or Legacy Executor permission in the app
Delete your account Settings > Delete account, or request deletion by email as described in the Account & Data Deletion Policy
Control AI eulogy permission Settings > Legacy Executor, before activation is locked
Withdraw AI consent for a Year in Review movie Each order requires fresh consent. Before production starts, use Cancel order in Year in Review to revoke consent and cancel that order. After production starts, email info@daycasion.com for assistance; a refund must be requested through Apple or Google.

2. Make a request to us

Send an email to info@daycasion.com from the address associated with your DayCasion account and describe whether you seek access, correction, a data copy, deletion, or information about processing. If you no longer control the address, explain that so we can agree on proportionate identity verification.

We aim to acknowledge requests promptly and respond within 30 days of verifying the request, unless a shorter legal period applies or a legally permitted extension is needed. We will explain any permitted extension. We may ask for proof of authority from an agent acting for someone else, and will not disclose another member's private memories solely because a request has been made.

If we deny a request, we will give the reason where permitted. You may appeal by replying with Appeal in the subject line; we will handle the appeal under applicable law and provide any required information about further remedies. Requests are generally free except when a legally permitted fee applies to a manifestly excessive request. We do not retaliate against members for exercising applicable privacy rights.

3. Rights that may apply where you live

Depending on where you live and which laws apply, you may have rights to know, access, correct, delete or receive a portable copy of personal information, and to appeal certain decisions. DayCasion does not sell personal information or operate targeted advertising, so it does not offer an opt-out for activities it does not undertake. We respond to valid requests under applicable law and offer the account controls described here to all members.

4. Information someone else added about you

DayCasion members can record memories that mention or show other people. Each member's account is private, and we generally cannot search other people's accounts on your behalf. If you believe a member stored information about you or your child without permission, or you have been named as a Legacy Executor and want your details removed, contact us. Tell us who you are and why you are writing; do not send copies of the material. We will review the request and may contact the account holder. We will act where the law or our Terms require.

5. Children's information

DayCasion accounts are intended only for people 18 or older. Adults may create Child profiles, subject to appropriate authority and consent. A parent or guardian concerned about content relating to a child in another member's account can contact info@daycasion.com; DayCasion will review the concern and any legal rights involved. If we discover a minor account holder, we may close the account following appropriate verification and legal requirements.

6. Deceased members

A named Legacy Executor may use only the authorization provided by the member through the Legacy Executor process. Other people requesting access to or deletion of an account belonging to a deceased person should email info@daycasion.com. We may require evidence of identity, death and the requester's legal authority; a personal or family relationship alone does not automatically grant access.

7. How we protect your information

These safeguards are in place today:

  • Encrypted (HTTPS) connections between the app, our servers and our providers.
  • Server-enforced access rules, so each account can reach only its own records and files.
  • Private file storage and access-controlled delivery. Some functions provide expiring signed links (currently configured at approximately 10 minutes for executor media, 1 hour for movie playback and 3 hours for rendering); recipients may retain copies they download.
  • Passwords, Legacy Codes and Legacy links stored only in protected (hashed) form.
  • Limits on Legacy Code attempts.
  • A 24-hour hold and stop link before any Legacy material is released.
  • Service credentials kept on our servers, never in the app.
  • No payment card details handled by DayCasion.

No system is perfectly secure. If a security incident affects your personal information, we will notify you and authorities as the law requires.

Limits of the automated export and deletion workflow

The quick export is not yet capable of delivering every very large account's media through self-service. Contact info@daycasion.com for help if your export is too large. DayCasion's explicit account-deletion process closes an account and runs monitored, retryable cleanup of stored records, media, derivative previews, exports and supported provider records. A confirmation email is issued only when the required cleanup has completed.

Account lapse versus a privacy-rights request

A subscription ending is not a deletion request. DayCasion has built—but not enabled—a 90-day automatic deletion lifecycle. Voluntary account deletion and qualifying privacy-rights requests use the separate verified account-deletion process; they do not require waiting for the optional subscription-expiration lifecycle.
