DayCasion AI Processing & Consent Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

This policy explains exactly when DayCasion uses artificial intelligence (AI), what information is involved, who processes it and how you control it. It supplements our Privacy Policy.

1. Our approach

  • DayCasion uses OpenAI for two optional features: Year in Review and, if you choose it, the AI-assisted Legacy Executor Eulogy. A completed eulogy you write yourself, and a video eulogy you upload, are never sent to AI.
  • Each Year in Review order requires fresh, affirmative AI-processing consent tied to that order. Consent from an earlier movie order is not reused. Eulogy AI permission is separate from Year in Review consent and from payment. It is requested only when you choose My Eulogy Information (AI-assisted) under My Eulogy (Settings > Legacy Executor, or import.daycasion.com), and is withdrawn when you choose No Eulogy or My Completed Eulogy (No AI).
  • For AI requests, we do not intentionally provide customer email addresses, payment-card details or account passwords.
  • DayCasion does not use customer memories to train its own models and does not intentionally authorize OpenAI to train models on this API content.
  • Basic memories, voice recordings, Life Stories, Add Past Memories, Desktop Import, Share to DayCasion, timelines and standard downloads do not use AI.

2. Year in Review

Service. A separately purchased movie for one memory profile, covering one of the last 10 calendar years or the most recent 12 months. The movie shows the selected photos and video clips (with their original sound) and on-screen text, and can include your original voice recordings, played over the photos of the memory they belong to. A voice recording is used only when its memory also has a photo. The movie has no added music or narrator.

What OpenAI receives: memory titles, written stories, dates and chapter names; the selected profile's name and category; relevant Life Story names and relationship; lower-resolution photo copies; the count and duration of voice and video recordings associated with each memory; whether a memory is marked must-include; and whether it belongs to a Life Story. OpenAI does not receive the original videos, voice or audio recordings, original full-resolution media, customer email, account credentials or payment information for this feature.

What OpenAI does: provides visual descriptions and assists with selecting memories and generating on-screen narrative text. These outputs may be imperfect. DayCasion performs automated checks and the finished movie is reviewed by an authorized person before delivery.

Shotstack rendering: Shotstack is a video-production service, not the AI story-writing provider. It receives links valid for approximately three hours to the chosen original photos, videos and audio recordings, and the on-screen text required to assemble the movie. Some render-request metadata may remain in Shotstack's own records under its terms after the hosted output is deleted.

Consent and choices: every movie order requires a separate, affirmative consent, recorded against that order with its disclosure version. No previous consent is reused. Before payment you can cancel without purchasing. After payment, while production has not started, the app offers Cancel order, which revokes that order's consent and stops its production. This cancellation window may last only a few minutes. Once processing has started, contact info@daycasion.com to request assistance. A paid-order cancellation does not automatically generate a refund; request a refund through Apple or Google, subject to their rules and applicable law. We cannot undo processing already completed by independent providers.

Storage: the produced movie, storyboard, generated descriptions and consent record remain private in DayCasion until removed or the account is deleted. DayCasion's account-deletion cleanup removes controlled movie and preview files; independent provider records may remain for their limited purposes.

3. Life Stories

Life Stories do not use AI. DayCasion does not transcribe recordings or write biographies with AI. If we offer such a feature in future, we will describe it here and ask for your separate, explicit permission first.

Information you record in a Life Story is used by AI only within the two features above:

  • Year in Review uses Life Story names and your stated relationship.
  • The AI-assisted Eulogy uses the Life Story of your My Life profile only if you switch Life Story on for it.

4. Legacy Executor and My Eulogy

Eulogy Only is an optional one-time, non-recurring purchase (the eulogy is also included in the Complete Legacy Package; Legacy Access on its own does not include one). Preparing your eulogy (uploading files or a video, or choosing an option) requires Eulogy Only or the Complete Legacy Package; without it you can still view the options and download the Eulogy Preparation Guide, and anything you prepared earlier is kept. Under My Eulogy you choose one of:

  • No Eulogy.
  • My Completed Eulogy (No AI). Your own finished eulogy (a PDF, Word or text file you upload at import.daycasion.com). It is never sent to OpenAI or any other AI, and DayCasion does not summarize, rewrite or change it. Your executor receives your words exactly as written, with a copy of your original document. No AI permission is needed.
  • My Eulogy Information (AI-assisted). Information you upload for your eulogy (the DayCasion Eulogy Preparation Guide or your own notes, up to three files) is used with AI to write your eulogy. This needs your explicit AI permission, which is separate from payment.
  • Personal Video Eulogy (No AI, optional). A video of up to 60 minutes (MP4 or MOV) that you upload at import.daycasion.com, in addition to either written choice or on its own. It is never sent to AI and is delivered exactly as you uploaded it. It uses your existing subscription storage (no extra storage is included and there is no extra fee), and you can replace or delete it until your legacy process begins.

AI permission for the AI-assisted eulogy:

  • When it is asked. When you choose My Eulogy Information (AI-assisted). It is not asked when you buy Eulogy Only or the Complete Legacy Package.
  • What it covers. Always the information files you upload for your eulogy. The written memories of your My Life profile and its Life Story are included only if you switch them on; switching one on asks for your permission again.
  • Withdrawing. Choosing No Eulogy or My Completed Eulogy (No AI) withdraws it straight away; you keep your purchase.
  • When it is used. A eulogy is written only if Eulogy Only or the Complete Legacy Package is purchased and the AI-assisted option, with its permission, is selected when the 24-hour stop period ends. Otherwise nothing is sent and no eulogy is written.

If the AI-assisted eulogy is chosen and permitted, and Eulogy Only or the Complete Legacy Package is purchased, DayCasion sends to OpenAI, after an authorized legacy activation and the 24-hour stop period: the text of your eulogy information files (your answers and notes, including the tone you asked for and the topics you asked to leave out) and your profile name, and, only if you switched them on, the written memories of your My Life profile (titles, stories, dates and chapter names) and its Life Story (names, relationship, birth details and other information you recorded). Original photos, video, audio recordings, your completed-eulogy file and other profiles' memories are never sent.

OpenAI writes a eulogy from this information. Automated controls remove statements that are not supported by what you provided (such as invented names, places, events or quotations) and statements touching topics you asked to leave out; the result may still contain errors and is not routinely reviewed by DayCasion staff before authorized delivery. Your executor receives only the finished eulogy, never your information files, answers or notes, including with Legacy Access. Your Legacy choices remain editable until the 24-hour stop period ends; after it ends, your choices are fixed for that legacy process.

Legacy Access does not use AI: it shares only the profiles and kinds of material you choose and save under Legacy Access, and nothing if you choose Don't share memories.

Your eulogy files are private and are kept and deleted with the rest of your account content under the same rules (see our Privacy Policy); there is no separate or longer retention for them. Deleting your account revokes remaining online executor access and deletes eulogy files and records controlled by DayCasion; it cannot erase copies already received by the executor.

5. How OpenAI handles the data

Our API requests are configured not to request stored responses for the features described above. OpenAI's applicable business-data terms generally exclude training on API content unless specifically authorized. Limited safety, abuse-monitoring or legal retention can still apply under the provider's terms, account settings and relevant endpoint behavior. For provider details, see OpenAI's API data controls.

6. Accuracy

AI-generated narratives can contain errors, omissions or interpretations that do not reflect your original memories. Contact info@daycasion.com about a delivered movie or eulogy that is inaccurate or upsetting. DayCasion may offer a correction or remake where feasible; movie refund requests are subject to the platform's rules and applicable law.

7. Other people in your memories

Your memories may include other people, including children. Before authorizing AI processing, consider your right to submit those materials and whether their information should appear in a movie. You control which profile and date period an order covers, and may mark up to 50 memories as must-include, subject to order eligibility.

8. Changes

If we add an AI feature, change AI providers, or send new kinds of information, we will update this policy. We will ask for your permission again before the change applies to you.

9. Contact

info@daycasion.com

Data minimization and processor roles

OpenAI receives low-resolution visual previews and the described text/metadata for story direction; Shotstack receives the original selected media through temporary URLs for rendering. The two providers serve different purposes, and consent must name both. An authorization for a eulogy does not authorize a movie, and consent to one movie must not be silently reused for another.

Errors, objection and paid-order remedies

For an undelivered Year in Review movie after production starts, contact info@daycasion.com promptly to request help or to object to further processing. We will review the production status, attempt a reasonable correction or remake where appropriate, and help you use Apple or Google refund channels if delivery cannot be completed. Provider processing already performed cannot necessarily be reversed. The Subscription, Refund & Digital Products Policy explains store refund procedures.
